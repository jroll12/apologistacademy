## Description (paste everything between the lines)

---

Most Christians want to share their faith, but when someone asks a hard question, like why God allows suffering, we freeze. Classes and books give us answers. They don't give us practice.

Apologist Academy is a safe place to practice. Pick one of sixteen hard questions, talk it through for five turns with an AI skeptic, ask Coach for help when you get stuck, and read a short review at the end. No scores, no accounts, nothing to install.

Try it free: https://apologistacademy.pages.dev/

How it works
• Pick a question: sixteen questions in eight topics, from suffering to science to other faiths.
• Choose your skeptic: curious, hostile or balanced, from gentle to tough.
• Talk for five turns: type or speak. The skeptic responds to what you actually said.
• Ask Coach: a hint first, then an outline, then a sample reply, so you find your own words.
• Read your review: what you covered, what you could add, one possible answer, and a verse to study.

Built in on purpose
• The AI is always labeled. The skeptic says it's simulated if you ask.
• The skeptic never claims to be converted and never insults you or your faith.
• If someone seems to be in crisis, it stops the exercise and points to real help.
• Coach quotes one checked verse (Berean Standard Bible) and never invents books, quotes or statistics.
• No scores. Conversations are saved only in your own browser.
