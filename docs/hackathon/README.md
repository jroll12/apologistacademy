## Shot list for re-recording on the live site (optional, about 2 minutes)

Use a screen recorder at 1920×1080 in a private browser window, so there's no history.

1. Home. Pause 3 seconds on the headline and tagline. Scroll down to the topics, then back up.
2. Press Start on the suggested question. On the question page, click Hostile, then Curious. Press Start.
3. Wait for the skeptic's opening. Type a sincere two-sentence reply and send it.
4. When the skeptic answers, press the AI button. Show the hint, then Show outline, then Show sample reply. Press Back to my reply.
5. Send a reply that asks the skeptic if the question is personal for them. Let the answer show for 3 seconds.
6. Send three short replies to reach five turns.
7. On the review, pause on What you covered, then scroll to Scripture. Open Your conversation. Show the Print or save as PDF link.
8. End on Home or the closing card from `demo.mp4`.
