# Demo script: full version (about 4 minutes 30 seconds)

For recording yourself on Loom or similar. The hackathon limit is 5 minutes, so this leaves a little room. Spoken lines are in plain quotes; what to do on screen is in brackets. Read it naturally, in your own words. The short, timed version for the captioned video is in `README.md`.

**Before you record**

- Use a private browser window at full screen, so there's no history and the first visit looks fresh.
- Run one practice conversation first on the live site, so you know the AI is working and roughly how it will answer.
- Have a good answer ready for the second turn and a personal question to ask the skeptic (below).
- Turn off notifications. Close other tabs.

---

## 1. The problem (0:00 to 0:35)

[Show Home, no scrolling yet.]

"Hi, I'm Jarret, and this is Apologist Academy, our entry for the AI for Church hackathon.

Here's the problem. Most Christians want to share their faith. But when a coworker, a neighbor or one of our kids asks a hard question, like why God allows suffering, we freeze. We know the material, but we've never practiced saying it out loud to someone who pushes back.

Classes and books give us answers. They don't give us practice. That's what this is."

## 2. Choosing a question (0:35 to 1:05)

[Scroll slowly to More questions. Point at the topic buttons. Scroll back up.]

"There are sixteen hard questions in eight topics: suffering, the Bible, Jesus, science, God, right and wrong, other faiths, and the church. You can start with the suggested one, pick a topic, search, or hit Surprise me.

Everything is private. There are no accounts. Your conversations are saved only in your own browser."

[Click Start on the suggested question.]

## 3. Choosing the skeptic (1:05 to 1:35)

[On the question page, click Hostile, then Balanced, then back to Curious.]

"Before you start, you choose who you're talking to. A Curious skeptic asks honest questions. A Hostile one pushes hard but stays civil. A Balanced one weighs both sides. You can also choose how hard it pushes, from gentle to tough.

The skeptic is always labeled as an AI, and if you ask it, it tells you it's a simulated skeptic. It's a practice partner, not a real person."

[Click Start. Wait for the opening line.]

## 4. The conversation (1:35 to 2:35)

[Read the skeptic's opening aloud.]

"The skeptic opens with the question. Now I reply, by typing or by speaking, with the mic button."

[Type a sincere two-sentence answer. Suggested: "That's a fair question, and I don't want to give you a cheap answer. I think part of it is that God made a world where love is real, and real love needs real choices." Send it.]

"The skeptic doesn't follow a script. It responds to what I actually said, and it will say so when I make a good point."

[Read its reply.]

"Notice the five bars at the top. A conversation is five turns, so there's a clear start and end."

## 5. Coach (2:35 to 3:25)

[Click the AI button.]

"Now say I'm stuck. I tap the AI button and Coach helps in steps. First, just a hint, one sentence that points at what the skeptic is really asking. No answer yet, because I want to find my own words first."

[Click Show outline.]

"If I still want help, an outline: a few points to cover."

[Click Show sample reply.]

"And last, a sample reply, in a natural voice. I can stop at any step. I'm not forced to use any of it."

[Click Back to my reply.]

## 6. Making it personal (3:25 to 3:50)

[Type: "Can I ask if this question is personal for you?" and send.]

"A good conversation isn't a debate to win. Here I ask the skeptic whether this is personal. A real person would answer, and the skeptic can too, in character."

[Read its answer. Then send quick replies to finish the five turns.]

## 7. The review (3:50 to 4:20)

[On the review page, pause on What you covered.]

"At the end, Coach writes a short review, written to me: what I did well, what I could add, and one possible answer. There are no scores. This is practice, not a test.

And there's always a verse to study, quoted from the Berean Standard Bible and checked word for word."

[Open Your conversation. Point at Print or save as PDF.]

"I can open the whole conversation, or print it or save it as a PDF to share with my small group."

## 8. Safeguards and close (4:20 to 4:50)

[Click How it works in the menu or top bar.]

"A few things we built in on purpose. The AI is always labeled. The skeptic never pretends to convert and never insults you or your faith. If someone seems to be in real distress, it stops the exercise and points to someone they can trust. Coach quotes only one checked verse and never invents books or statistics. And the whole thing is private by design.

We built this with the Apologist Project's AI platform to help ordinary believers be ready, with gentleness and respect, to give an answer.

Thanks for watching. You can try it yourself at the link below."

[End on Home or the title card.]

---

## If you run short on time

Cut sections 3 and 6. The story still holds: problem, practice, Coach, review, safeguards.

## If the AI is slow or the review shows general notes

Say so honestly and keep going: "Sometimes the AI is slow. When it is, the app falls back to written notes for the question so you're never stuck." Then try again once. Don't re-record just for this.
