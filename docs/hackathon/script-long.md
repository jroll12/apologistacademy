# Demo script with slides (about 4 minutes 45 seconds)

For recording yourself in one take. The hackathon limit is 5 minutes. You switch between two browser tabs: **the slides** (`slides/slides.html`) and **the app** (https://apologistacademy.pages.dev/).

- **[SLIDE n]**: switch to the slides tab and show slide n.
- **[APP]**: switch to the app tab.
- Spoken lines are in quotes. Say them in your own words.
- ★ marks a slide you can skip if you're running long.

## Before you record

- **Two tabs, one window.** Tab 1: open `slides.html` (double-click the file; it opens in your browser). Tab 2: the app in a **private window with extensions off**, so the first-run "How it works" screen and the random suggestion behave as a new visitor's would. Put both tabs in the same window, so Cmd+1 and Cmd+2 (Ctrl+1 and Ctrl+2 on Windows) jump between them.
- **Slides:** right arrow or space for the next slide, left arrow to go back, F for full screen (Esc leaves it). On slide 10, the live phone takes clicks; click anywhere outside the phone before you press the arrow again.
- **Do one full practice run** on the live site first, so you know the AI is answering and roughly how long it takes.
- **Have your five replies ready to paste** (at the bottom of this page). Typing on camera takes too long. Say "I've pasted my replies in to save time."
- **Record at 1920×1080**, notifications off, other tabs closed. On a Mac, Cmd+Shift+5 records the screen for free. Loom works too, but check its free plan's length limit first (it has been 5 minutes).
- The slides need internet for their fonts and the live phone. Everything else is in the file.

## Part 2: the live demo (1:25 to 3:45)

**[APP] Home** (1:25). If "How it works" opens, press Got it.

"There are no accounts. There are sixteen questions in eight topics, and on a phone you swipe through them." [Scroll down, swipe or scroll the topic buttons, then click "Why does God allow suffering?"]

**[APP] The question page** (1:40)

"First I choose who I'm talking to. Curious asks honest questions. Hostile pushes hard but stays civil. Balanced weighs both sides. And I set how hard it pushes." [Click Hostile, then back to Curious. Click Start.]

**[APP] The conversation** (1:55). Read the skeptic's opening line aloud. Paste reply 1 and send.

"I reply by typing or by speaking with the mic. While it thinks, here's the same thing on a phone."

**[SLIDE 9] Built for your phone** (2:05)

"Most of these conversations happen on a phone, so it's built for one first: swipe through topics, type or speak, and Coach slides up from the bottom."

**[APP]** (2:15). Read the skeptic's reply.

"It doesn't follow a script. It answers what I actually said, and if I dodge its question, it calls that out."

**[APP] Coach** (2:25). Click the AI button.

"Say I'm stuck. Coach helps in steps. First a hint, so I find my own words." [Show outline.] "Then an outline." [Show sample reply.] "Then a sample reply. I can stop at any step." [Back to my reply.]

**[APP] Making it personal** (2:50). Paste reply 2, which asks if the question is personal, and send.

"A good conversation isn't a debate to win. Here I ask if this is personal, and the skeptic answers in character." [Read its answer.]

**[APP] Finish the five turns** (3:05). Paste and send replies 3, 4 and 5. While you wait:

"Five turns gives a clear start and a clear end. And replies can be long, up to five thousand characters, so I can quote Scripture."

**[SLIDE 11] Safeguards** (3:25). Switch the moment the page says "Coach is writing your review." It takes 10 to 15 seconds.

"While Coach writes my review, here's what we built in on purpose. The AI is always labeled. The skeptic never pretends to be converted and never insults you. If someone seems to be in crisis, it stops and points to real help. Coach quotes one checked verse and never invents sources. And there are no scores and no accounts."

**[APP] The review** (3:45)

"Here's my review: what I covered, what I could add, one possible answer, and a verse from the Berean Standard Bible." [Scroll a little. Open Your conversation, point at Print or save as PDF.] "I can print the whole conversation to bring to my small group."

If the review shows general notes instead: "When the AI is slow, it falls back to written notes, so you're never stuck." Keep going.

## If you're running long

The spoken lines alone take about 3 minutes 50 seconds at a normal pace; clicks and AI waits make up the rest. If your practice run goes past 4:50, skip the ★ slides (4, 7, 13 and 14) to save about 30 seconds. If you're still long, skip "Making it personal" and send reply 2 without talking over it.

## Replies to paste

1. That's a fair question, and I don't want to give you a cheap answer. I think part of it is that God made a world where love is real, and real love needs real choices.
2. You're right, that doesn't explain all of it. What I hold on to is that God didn't stay far from pain. In Jesus he stepped into it. Can I ask if this question is personal for you?
3. If it is personal, I'm sorry. I don't know why God allows each thing. I do know Jesus wept at a friend's grave. Pain matters to him.
4. I'd rather sit with you in it than explain it away.
5. Mostly, I'd listen. And I'd say the cross means God knows suffering from the inside, and it won't have the last word.
